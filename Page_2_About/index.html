Our Story

Spice Junction was born in 2005 from a simple dream — to bring the soulful, home-cooked flavours of regional India to the heart of Chennai. Our founder, Arjun Krishnamurthy, grew up watching his grandmother grind fresh masalas every morning in their Coimbatore home, and that memory never left him.

What started as a modest 20-seat eatery quickly grew into a beloved landmark. Today, Spice Junction seats over 120 guests and has become a go-to destination for families and food lovers who crave honest, hearty Indian meals.

Every dish is made with spices sourced directly from farms in Kerala and Rajasthan, recipes refined over twenty years, and a kitchen team that genuinely loves what they cook. We don't believe in shortcuts — and our guests can always taste the difference.
